How to share AI answers and Markdown in a chat

Turn Markdown from ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Codex or Cursor into editable text and images, with clear limits for each chat client.

Content reviewed：2026-10-18

Direct answer

Copy the Markdown source from your AI tool, paste it into md2chat, check the preview and copy for your destination. Ordinary text remains editable; tables, code blocks, equations and Mermaid diagrams become images. The named AI tools are examples of content sources, not official integrations.

Why does formatting change after copying?

An AI interface renders Markdown, while its copy button and text selection may expose different clipboard formats. The destination also chooses which formats to import. A correct preview does not prove that a chat client will retain every image.

What should I copy?

Keep the original Markdown table, LaTeX equation source and fenced mermaid code. If the source only contains a placeholder or the rendered text of a missing equation, md2chat cannot reconstruct it. No AI account connection is required.

Choose the destination workflow

DingTalk and Feishu copying require the desktop app. Expand the rich-text input in Feishu. With images, WeCom uses sequential text and PNG copies. WeChat uses standard HTML; email remains experimental. Check the platform guide before sending.

Step by step

  1. Copy Markdown from your source and paste it into md2chat.
  2. Check the text, table image and final paragraph in the preview.
  3. Choose your destination and inspect the result in your own draft.

A public example

# Weekend book club

Start: see you on Saturday.

| Activity | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Quiet reading | 20 minutes |
| Discussion | 30 minutes |

End: remember to bring a book.

Check the opening text, table image and closing text in your own draft. Ordinary text should remain editable.
